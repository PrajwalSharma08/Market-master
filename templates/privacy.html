Privacy Policy for Market Master Pro

Effective Date: August 5, 2026

1. Overview

Market Master Pro is a live market charting and quantitative analytical trading dashboard. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting user data.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Market Master Pro does not collect, store, or sell any personal data, financial details, account credentials, or device contacts. All market charts and data feeds are fetched publicly from financial market data providers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

The application uses browser localStorage solely to save your local dashboard layout preferences (such as your chosen number of chart panes and default symbols).

4. Third-Party Data Providers

Public market information is retrieved from Yahoo Finance and Hyperliquid public WebSockets. No personal identifiers are transmitted during these requests.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at support@marketmaster.app.
